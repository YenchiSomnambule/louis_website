01

這些情況你認得嗎？Does any of this sound familiar?

  • 每個月有人花兩三天，把幾個系統的數字抄進同一份報表。Every month, someone spends two or three days copying numbers out of several systems into one report.
  • 兩套系統各有一份客戶資料，對不起來，也沒人確定哪一份是對的。Two systems hold two versions of the customer list, they don't reconcile, and nobody is sure which one is right.
  • 信件和表單要有人先讀過，才知道該轉給誰、該不該急。Emails and form submissions have to be read by a person before anyone knows who they go to or how urgent they are.
  • 某個流程只有一個人會跑，因為它只存在他的腦袋裡。One process only one person can run, because it exists only in their head.
  • 系統請求量或授權費一直漲，但沒人知道是哪些流程在吃。System request volume or licence costs keep climbing and nobody can say which flows are eating them.
  • 想用 AI，但不確定從哪個環節開始才會真的省到錢。You want to use AI but aren't sure which step to start with so that it actually saves money.

這些都不是「再努力一點」能解決的問題，它們是系統之間沒有接起來造成的。接起來之後，那些時間就回到你的團隊手上。 None of these are fixed by working harder. They happen because the systems aren't connected. Connect them and the hours go back to your team.

05

什麼情況適合找我When this is a fit, and when it isn't

適合A good fit

  • 已經有 CRM、ERP、表單或試算表在跑，但它們彼此不說話。You already run on a CRM, an ERP, forms or spreadsheets, and they don't talk to each other.
  • 有固定、重複、規則講得清楚的人工作業。There is regular, repetitive work whose rules can be written down.
  • 有一位窗口能回答業務問題，不必懂技術。One person can answer questions about the business. They don't need to be technical.
  • 你想要的是能交接的系統，不是長期綁定的顧問。You want a system you can take over, not a consultant you stay tied to.

不適合Not a fit

  • 想一次把整間公司數位轉型。範圍太大，先從一條流程開始。You want to transform the whole company at once. That's too broad — start with one flow.
  • 流程還在每週改，規則尚未定案。自動化會鎖住一個還在動的東西。The process still changes weekly. Automating it would freeze something that is still moving.
  • 希望 AI 完全不需要人審核。做得到，但風險應該由你自己評估後決定，而不是預設。You want AI with no human check at all. It can be built, but that should be your decision after weighing the risk, not the default.
  • 需要現場駐點。我是遠端工作。You need someone on site. I work remotely.
